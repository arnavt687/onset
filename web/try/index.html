Try Onset in your browser

A one-minute check-in for the 90 days after a stroke

Onset is a FaceTime call that runs the FAST checks and compares them with the person's own usual check-in. This is the same check on your webcam, in about a minute.

  1. 1
    SmileRelax your face, then a big smile.
  2. 2
    ArmsBoth arms straight out for 10 seconds.
  3. 3
    SentenceRepeat one short sentence out loud.

Your camera stays in this browser. The AI runs on your device, and Onset records and uploads nothing.

A demo of the experience, not a medical test.
